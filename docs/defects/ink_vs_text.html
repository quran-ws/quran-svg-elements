Where quran-ws and the ink disagree

quran-ws/quran-text v3.0 is the text of record we are moving to. This is the complete list of places where it does not describe the ink this mushaf actually draws — 2 words out of 77,432, checked across 331,129 mark elements. Everything else agrees, once encoding is read as encoding rather than as content.

Four conventions are normalised first, or they drown the real signal: the five waqf signs get one name each rather than one bucket; the KFGQPC alphabet (U+0652 as the rounded zero, U+0656/7/E as the open tanwin); the precomposed آ U+0622, which carries its maddah inside the letter where rasm_uthmani decomposes it; and quran-ws's marks[] layer, of which ONLY the waqf kind belongs back in the word — folding in its hizb (199) and sajdah (15) kinds too put a ۞ on the first word of 199 ayahs and made them look like word-boundary errors. A further 14 words genuinely key to a different word on each side (9:100, 72:16, 11:41, 2:72) and need boundaries reconciled, not marks.

These are the words where adopting quran-ws would change a mark budget, so each one needs your verdict. The image is the real ink, cropped from the page it is drawn on; teal is the current text, red the two KFGQPC releases (v2.0 as carried in data-qpc, and yours). They flag identically here, though they are not the same text elsewhere.
