86:1:1وَٱلسَّمَآءِ
Reported
the kasra is under the tag data-text="وا" not "لسما", and that group now holds لسماء not لسما — either move the kasra and rename, or give the hamza its own group with its kasra. How many similar cases are in the data?
What it was
two separate faults. The first is mine: the four passes I added this session appended a moved mark to `at[0]`, the word's FIRST ligature group, instead of the group whose ink it sits over — `_omove`, which every older mover uses, picks the nearest atom. The second predates them: `segment_word` cuts وَٱلسَّمَآءِ into ['و','ا','لسما','ء'] and the hamza's ink is emitted inside the `لسما` group, leaving `ء` empty.
Where it stands
Routing fixed — all four passes now call `put_in_ligature()`, the same nearest-atom rule, so the kasra lands with the hamza. The empty `ء` group is a real defect and 18 words share it; the audit that finds them is now `tools/audit_ligatures.py`. Correcting it is not written.

14no single page
Reported
many diacritics show as (unlabeled) now; fixing those is a priority that could fix many issues
What it was
a display bug in the review platform, not a defect in the decomposition. It counted `path[data-kind="mark"]:not([data-mark])` as unlabeled, but a composite mark is labelled ONCE on its master and its other pieces carry `data-mark-part` instead.
Where it stands
14,855 welded pieces were being shown in red as needing an answer. By the pipeline's own reckoning only 22 marks in the whole mushaf are genuinely unnamed — 0.005% of 436,743. The platform now colours a piece like its master at 55% opacity and stops asking for it.
