Every path here is relative, so the demo works over this address, over the tailnet, or straight off a copy of the folder — no rebuild needed. The pages are the production profile: one group per word, no ligature groups, no ayah polygons, and none of the review-loop attributes.

On the sign catalogue. It was read out of the King Fahd Complex's own PDF of the 1441 AH printing, while the artwork here is the 1441 H V2 print. The sign system is the same and the catalogue is the best description of it we have — but where the two ever disagree, the ink in pages/ is what this project treats as authoritative, because every page is verified pixel-identical to it.
